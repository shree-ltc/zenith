Core Systems

Engineered for absolute resilience, zero downtime, and zero privilege escalation.

[SECURITY] MAINROLE GUARD

Privilege Escalation Shield

Protects @everyone and verified member roles from receiving dangerous administrative permissions. Intercepts permission grants on the gateway and strips them within 0.5s back to safe states.

[DEFENSE] REAL-TIME ANTINUKE

Autonomous Raid Defense

Monitors audit logs for unauthorized channel deletions, role tampering, rogue bot additions, and member purges. Instantly neutralizes offending actors and reverts destructive actions.

[VELOCITY] ANTI-BETRAY PROTOCOL

Compromised Account Mitigation

Tracks moderator velocity in rolling time windows. When administrative actions exceed safety thresholds, the actor is immediately stripped of staff roles and quarantined.

[MODERATION] FAKE PERMISSIONS

Zero-Trust Staff Authorization

Grants Zenith moderation authority to staff roles without assigning native Discord permissions, guarded by daily action quotas to prevent token misuse or moderator abuse.

[PURIFICATION] PURGE MATRIX

Target-Specific Message Purge

15 dedicated purification commands designed for clean message sweeps: bots, links, embeds, attachments, mentions, reactions, and specific users with safe rate limits.

[STREAMING] AUDIO ENGINE

Lavalink & Smart Autoplay

High-fidelity audio streaming integrated with YouTube RD Mix and parallel artist discovery that continuously queues non-repeating related tracks.
