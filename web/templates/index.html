[SECURITY] MAINROLE GUARD

Privilege Escalation Shield

Guards default @everyone and verified member roles from receiving dangerous administrative permissions. Intercepts permission grants on the gateway and strips them within 0.5s back to safe states.

[ANTINUKE] GATEWAY DEFENSE

Autonomous Raid Defense

Monitors audit logs for unauthorized channel deletions, role tampering, rogue bot additions, and member purges. Instantly neutralizes offending actors and reverts destructive actions.

[VELOCITY] ANTI-BETRAY SHIELD

Compromised Staff Mitigation

Tracks moderator velocity in rolling time windows. When administrative actions exceed safety thresholds, the actor is immediately stripped of staff roles and quarantined.
